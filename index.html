Bio #1

Robert Atkinson is an artist living in Albuquerque, New Mexico, blending traditional mediums like drawing and painting with digital tools and the evolving power of Artificial Intelligence. Every tool and process he uses complements and builds on the other, sometimes blending them seamlessly together.

With experience providing graphic design services across a wide range of business needs, as well as commissioned murals, paintings, and physical art pieces, he now focuses on helping new startups build memorable and meaningful brands. He works with emerging companies to establish strong visual identities from the beginning, creating consistent brand systems that carry across every customer touchpoint. By developing unified branding early and supporting it with ongoing on-brand messaging, he helps companies build trust, strengthen their reputation, and grow with confidence.

His personal art style is evolving as he begins tp implement vibe coding, agentic AI,, new media platforms and automated systems into his work. Now, he can build immersive, interactive experiences that allow people to engage personally with his art, rather than simply look at it.

With these tools, whole new dimensions are possible. large themes sculptures begin to have two way conversations, murals become animated, and paintings become puzzles people can solve in real time.

Robert is inspired by the possibility these tools make possible, and he is driven to develop new artforms that have never before been possible on Planet Earth.

Bio #2

Robert Atkinson is an artist living in Albuquerque, New Mexico, blending traditional mediums like drawing and painting with digital tools and the evolving power of Artificial Intelligence. Every tool and process he uses compliments and builds on the others, sometimes blending them seamlessly together.

But those are not the things he is known for…

In 2019, disturbed by a rise in "Karen" videos being posted on social media, he founded a non-profit organization to discover and turn off the gene that is responsible for one’s urge to complain aloud in public. This was a feat that was recognized globally, reminding the World’s population that solutions are out there to be discovered. He had reoriented Earth toward a more peaceful planet, and in 2022, it earned him a second Nobel Peace Prize nomination. Honored by the nod, he felt the competition wasn’t fair to his co-laureates, and voluntarily resigned from consideration. In a later interview he would say, “What am I gonna do with two of those things?” Then he vanished into thin air.

This is not to say everything he is involved in is an altruistic and selfless pursuit. He makes billions from his many companies that develop things like:

  • Transdermal patches that makes your kids smarter
  • Nanobots that are injected in a person’s arm and it instantly restores their hope in humanity
  • A digital pulsing bracelet that makes it impossible to be grumpy
  • Human teleportation (details still not published)
  • A radio signal that summons alien craft
  • And a stem cell technology that stops bad posture in alpacas.

Somehow, with an unimaginably packed schedule, he still finds time to enjoy his hobbies:

  • Playing the cello in the London Symphony Orchestra and at senior citizen’s birthday parties,
  • Donating time to improve the paintings at the Sistine Chapel
  • Being Elon’s personal tutor, explaining some of the more technical aspects of rocket propulsion
  • And training his three Irish Setters how to knit winter caps for a local homeless shelter.

Some say he is a genius. Some say he represents the Human Race’s next evolutionary step. Still others say they feel he is sent as a gift from a superior race to bring human beings closer together. No one could possibly know who or what he actually is, we just know we’re grateful for his contributions to society.

When he was asked, “Robert, your accomplishments are astonishing! Do these things come easy to you?”

He simply replied, “Um…Sorta.” Then he vanished from sight.
